Section 02 — Inside the book

The BUILD Framework.

This isn't a book about what AI will do to Africa. It's about what Africans will build with it — five pillars, one strategy.

01
B

Build African Capability

Skills, research, institutions, and datasets in African languages.

02
U

Use AI to Solve African Problems

Education, healthcare, agriculture, and jobs — not just chatbots.

03
I

Invest in African Innovation

Capital, compute, data centers, and the infrastructure underneath.

04
L

Lead Responsibly

Governance, ethics, data sovereignty, and policy that protects.

05
D

Develop African Ownership

IP, companies, platforms, and customer relationships that stay.

Hippolyte Asah

AI Leader · Entrepreneur · Author · Speaker

Toronto-based technology professional with nearly two decades in software architecture, cloud, DevOps, and AI. Founder of JobsAI — AI platforms for employment and workforce productivity. Host of The Smart Office Podcast on Spotify, YouTube, iHeartRadio, and Amazon Music.
