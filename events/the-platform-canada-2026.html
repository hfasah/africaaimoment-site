Why this talk

Algorithms trained on data that leaves people out make decisions that leave people out. Africa's AI Moment argues that Africa should help build, govern, and own the technologies shaping its future, not only consume them. This talk takes that argument to equity: where algorithmic bias comes from, and what the people who build and deploy AI can do about it.

About the speaker

Hippolyte Asah is a technology leader, AI implementation specialist, engineer, entrepreneur, author, and community builder, with nearly two decades in software architecture, cloud, DevOps, and AI. Founder of JobsAI, host of The Smart Office Podcast, and author of Africa's AI Moment: From Consumers of Technology to Creators of the Future.
